# Ethics-First Design Philosophy

The ethics-first design philosophy prioritizes ethical considerations in the design and implementation of technology. This philosophy is founded on several key principles:

## Design Principles

### 1. Transparency
   Users should have clear visibility into how the technology operates and the processes behind it. This includes making the design choices, data handling processes, and decision-making algorithms comprehensible to users.

### 2. Tamper-Resistance
   Systems should be designed to prevent unauthorized alterations and ensure data integrity. This protects users from malicious attacks and ensures that the information remains trustworthy.

### 3. Local-First Processing
   Data processing should occur locally on the user's device whenever possible. This minimizes data transfer and enhances privacy by keeping sensitive information out of central servers.

### 4. Accountability
   Designers and developers must take responsibility for the technology they create. This involves establishing clear accountability mechanisms to address potential failures or misuse of the system.

### 5. Open Feedback
   Continuous improvement of technology must be supported by open channels for user feedback. Engaging users in the design process fosters a collaborative environment and allows for adjustments based on real-world use cases.

## Key Rejections

In establishing ethical boundaries, certain approaches are intentionally rejected:

- **Optical Character Recognition (OCR)**: This technology can compromise user privacy and security by extracting sensitive information from documents without explicit consent. Thus, it is rejected in favor of methods that respect user autonomy.

- **Self-Signed Verification**: While this may offer a sense of authenticity, it lacks external validation and can lead to misuse. Emphasis is placed on trusted third-party verification to maintain integrity and trustworthiness.

By adhering to these principles and ethical boundaries, we foster a technology landscape that upholds the values of respect, integrity, and user empowerment.
