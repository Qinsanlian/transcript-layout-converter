Bulk course import

Single-term mode hides the second term. Use Term season order below to control which semester appears first (Fall then Spring, or Spring then Fall).

GPA inputs

Only Percent and Credits enter the GPA calculation. Edits update Grade Pt, Grade, and Quality Pts per the ladder (quality pts = grade pt × credits earned).

Non-calculated columns

Course Type / Course Number / Description are descriptive only and excluded from formulas.

Per term

  • Attempted / Earned / GPA Hours = sum of credits earned (identical here).
  • Quality Points = sum of row Quality Pts.
  • Term GPA = Quality Points ÷ GPA Hours.

Cumulative

  • Carry term totals forward in semester order.
  • Cumulative GPA = cumulative Quality Points ÷ cumulative GPA Hours.
