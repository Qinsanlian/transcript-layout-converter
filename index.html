        Privacy, blur preview, and local audit log
        

          
Privacy

          

            Work in a private environment. All processing is local to this browser (comparable to editing a sensitive PDF offline).
          

          

            On a shared workstation, clear site data for this origin after use (typically under Cookies or site settings).
          

        

        

          
Local audit log

          

            May retain hashed event metadata (version, acknowledgments, edits, exports) for your own accountability trail. No network upload. Clear site data to remove.
          

          

            The 18-digit field supports layout and a local hash watermark; logs store hashes only, not plaintext. Enter digits you accept on the printed layout; avoid sensitive values on public terminals.

GPA inputs

            
Only Percent and Credits enter the GPA calculation. Edits update Grade Pt, Grade, and Quality Pts per the ladder (quality pts = grade pt × credits earned).

          

          

            
Non-calculated columns

            
Course Type / Course Number / Description are descriptive only and excluded from formulas.

          

          

            
Per term

            
    
              
  • Attempted / Earned / GPA Hours = sum of credits earned (identical here).
    • 
              
  • Quality Points = sum of row Quality Pts.
    • 
              
  • Term GPA = Quality Points ÷ GPA Hours.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Cumulative

            
    
              
  • Carry term totals forward in semester order.
    • 
              
  • Cumulative GPA = cumulative Quality Points ÷ cumulative GPA Hours.

        Security block, UTC & QR (matches transcript; author notes)
        

          
Audience. On-transcript security copy is for reviewers; this foldout is for you as author—semantics, limits, and fixes.

          
Timestamps. Fingerprint and PNG-queue lines use the browser local clock. UTC reference is taken at export from public time sources; not legal or institutional attestation.

          
UTC detail. Parallel public sources, first success; if all fail, labeled browser UTC. Reconcile on local timestamps + content hash (not a substitute for issuer records).

          
Print cues. Microprint, watermark, VOID tiling—visual screening only; official stock and seals follow the issuer.

          
QR. Export-bound snapshot (sidebar fields, transcript HTML digest, compliance log refs)—file ↔ export pairing; supplementary to CHSI/registrar checks only. Blank QR area: see below.

          

          

            
QR library missing: place qrcode.min.js (not qrious) next to index.html and open via HTTP (not file://).

            
QR render blocked: tracking protection or strict canvas policies may prevent drawing. Try relaxing protections for this origin, allowlisting the site, or serving from 127.0.0.1 / localhost.
